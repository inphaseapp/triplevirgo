# Vision

Canonical company vision & North Star. North Star section lives on `/triplevirgo`.

---

A world where technology deepens self-awareness, strengthens relationships, and creates greater understanding between people.

## Our North Star

We don’t build technology to capture attention.  
We build technology to cultivate awareness.

Every product we create has the power to leave people feeling more connected to themselves than when they first opened it.

## Platform future vision (Phase products)

From platform constitution — for product planning, not company-site hero copy:

- Support a lifetime of relationships
- Guidance for spouse, children, parents, siblings, friends, mentors, and colleagues
- Every relationship should feel unique and generate its own intelligence
- Closing belief: not another relationship app — the world’s most thoughtful relationship intelligence platform
