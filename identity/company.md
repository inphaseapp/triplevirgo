**Reasoning (name story):** The name comes from the founder’s natal chart — Sun, Rising, and Venus in Virgo — a reminder that curiosity, thoughtful design, and careful observation can become acts of service. Full narrative: [`founder.md`](./founder.md) and live page `/triplevirgo`.

## What the company is

TripleVirgo is the **company** behind the products — not an app landing page. The public site should leave the impression: *These people build technology with intention.* Apps are luminous points; **philosophy is the hero.**

**One-line / lockup tagline:** Building technology that inspires understanding.

**Company site SEO description (live):** TripleVirgo creates thoughtfully designed technology that helps people better understand themselves and each other.

## Core question

> How can technology help us better understand ourselves and each other?

## Brand mantra

> The background whispers. The details shimmer. The logo sings.

## Feeling & references

**Intended feeling:** Apple meets sacred geometry, luxury wellness, and the night sky — a beautiful mystical walk at dusk.

**Emotions:** calm, cosmic, beautiful, luxurious, peaceful, timeless, spacious, intelligent, ethereal, reflective.

**References named in brief:** Apple, Aesop, Notion, COSMOS, luxury editorial — not typical SaaS. Later reinforced: *Think Apple. Think Hermès. Think Aesop. Luxury is space.*

## Visual journey (company surfaces)

**Night → Dusk.** Do not make the whole company site a dark night sky.

- **Night** — reserved for the hero / cosmos entry
- **Dusk** — the rest of the experience: softer, calmer, more grounded (reflection → human experience)

Meaning of the scroll: Cosmos → Reflection → Human experience.

Full site rules: [`website.md`](./website.md). Palette: [`colors/`](./colors/).
