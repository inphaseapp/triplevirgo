# Impact

Canonical intended impact. Derived from company philosophy, North Star, and platform founding principle — not a separate founder brief with metrics.

---

TripleVirgo exists to make understanding more accessible — through calm, intentional technology that supports reflection, connection, and growth.

## Intended impact

- Help people understand their own rhythms and inner world
- Help partners and relationships communicate with greater clarity
- Encourage presence over distraction
- Treat design as an act of care and service

## Measure of success

Not time spent in-app.

Whether someone leaves feeling more understood — by themselves or by someone they love.

Aligned with platform founding principle: if removing the app would leave behind better communication, greater understanding, and more thoughtful habits, the feature is successful. Transformation, not engagement.
