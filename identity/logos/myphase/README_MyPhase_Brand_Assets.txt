MYPHASE BRAND ASSETS
====================

SOURCE OF TRUTH
The gemstone emblem and presentation board in 01_Master and 06_Brand_Reference
are preserved from the original MyPhase artwork. No logo regeneration was used.

CORE MARK
Two polished gemstone crescents:
- Left: White Moonstone / warm pearl
- Right: Rose Quartz / milky blush pink

TAGLINE
Know yourself. Love yourself.

OFFICIAL PALETTE
Pearl       #F7F3F0
Warm Ivory  #F0E6E2
Blush       #F4D8DF
Dusty Rose  #E7B7BE
Mauve Gray  #9E8E93

FOLDER GUIDE
01_Master
  Original standalone emblem, unchanged.

02_Transparent
  Transparent-background emblem and logo lockup.

03_Backgrounds
  Large presentation-ready emblem files on Pearl, Warm Ivory, and Charcoal.

04_Web_Social
  Ready-to-use square PNGs for websites, profile images, avatars, and favicons.

05_Press
  3000px transparent emblem plus dark and light logo lockups.

06_Brand_Reference
  Original brand presentation showing the approved visual treatment, tagline,
  gemstone materials, app-icon concept, and palette.

USAGE
- Prefer the transparent emblem for flexible layouts.
- Use the Charcoal version when a finished square avatar is needed.
- Use the supplied dark/light lockups when the MyPhase name and tagline should
  appear together.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, separate, or redraw the gemstone crescents.
- Maintain clear space around the mark.
- For very small uses, use the emblem without the wordmark/tagline.

NOTE
The original source artwork is raster PNG, not vector artwork. These files
preserve that artwork rather than tracing or recreating it.
