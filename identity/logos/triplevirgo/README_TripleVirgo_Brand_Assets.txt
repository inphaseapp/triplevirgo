TRIPLE VIRGO BRAND ASSETS
=========================

SOURCE OF TRUTH
These assets were created from the approved TripleVirgo logo supplied in the
TripleVirgo project conversation. The logo artwork itself was not regenerated.

APPROVED FULL LOGO
- Sacred geometric triangle / orbital mark
- Three luminous vertex stars
- triplevirgo wordmark
- Tagline: BUILDING TECHNOLOGY THAT INSPIRES UNDERSTANDING.

FOLDER GUIDE
01_Master
  Original approved JPEG, unchanged.

02_Transparent
  Background-removed versions of the full logo and sacred mark.

04_Web_Social
  Ready-to-use square files in common website, favicon, and social sizes.

05_Press
  Original and high-resolution full-logo files.

06_Brand_Reference
  Source crop of the sacred mark for reference.

USAGE
- Treat the master logo as the source of truth.
- Do not redraw or alter the sacred geometry.
- Do not move or change the three luminous stars.
- Do not stretch, rotate, or recolor the approved mark.
- Use the mark-only files for small avatars/favicons.
- Use the full logo when the brand name and tagline should be visible.

NOTE
The source supplied here is raster JPEG artwork, not a vector master.
Transparent versions are carefully background-keyed derivatives of that raster
source; retain the original master for archival and press reference.
